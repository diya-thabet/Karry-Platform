# P2.13 public landing page (Karry website): slice plan

> [Docs index](../../README.md) › [04-phases](../README.md) › [Phase 2 plan](plan.md) · [Sub-phases](sub-phases.md) · [Tracker](tracker.md)

**In plain words:** the page people see when they type Karry's address. It explains what Karry does for a quarry in
seconds, looks premium (motion, depth, a 3D hero), works in French, English and Arabic (right to left), and has
**Se connecter** and **Créer un compte** at the top right. Sign-in leads to the Karry sign-in page restyled in November
2026 (`deploy/keycloak/themes/karry/login`). It is a sub-phase of Phase 2 added at the owner's request; it does not
change the M2 exit gate.

## 1. Goals and measures

| Goal | Measure (checked in CI) |
|---|---|
| Understood in 5 seconds | The hero says what Karry is and for whom, with one primary action; 5-second test with 5 people (owner) |
| Premium and memorable | Animated hero with the Karry mark in 3D, scroll-revealed sections, micro-interactions; design review by the owner on a desktop and a phone |
| Fast | Lighthouse (mobile, throttled): Performance ≥ 90, LCP < 2.5 s, CLS < 0.1, TBT < 200 ms; first-load JS ≤ 120 kB gzip before the 3D scene, which loads after the first paint |
| Accessible | WCAG 2.2 AA: axe with no serious or critical violation on every page and language; keyboard-only navigation; every animation off under `prefers-reduced-motion` |
| Three languages | French (default), English, Arabic (RTL mirrored); a language switch that keeps the page; `hreflang` and `<html lang dir>` correct |
| Found | Prerendered HTML per language (no blank page for crawlers), title and description per page, Open Graph image, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` |
| Safe | The same page headers as the apps (CSP without `unsafe-eval`, `frame-ancestors 'none'`, HSTS, `nosniff`); no third-party tracker; ZAP baseline added to the nightly scan |

## 2. Content (the page, top to bottom)

1. **Top bar:** the Karry logo (`karry-logo.svg`), links to the sections, the language switch (FR · EN · ع),
   **Se connecter** (secondary) and **Créer un compte** (primary). On a phone: logo, menu button, the two actions in
   the menu. The bar condenses and gains a glass background when the page scrolls.
2. **Hero:** headline ("Du terrain au bureau, des saisies sûres."), one sentence, the two actions; on the side a 3D
   Karry mark (the K and its rising arrow) floating and catching the light, turning slightly with the pointer, with
   the facets and rising arrows of the sign-in page behind it. Without WebGL, or with reduced motion: the animated SVG
   logo of the sign-in page, still.
3. **Trust strip:** the guarantees in short (signed on the tablet, works offline, verifiable by QR, data per company).
4. **How it works:** three steps with illustrations that animate on scroll: the operator records the shift on the
   tablet (photo, signature) → the controller reviews in the portal (photos side by side, alerts) → the signed report
   and its QR code anyone can check.
5. **Features:** cards for offline tablets, meter continuity and integrity alerts, the review queue, signed PDF
   reports, the audit trail, languages and right to left. Each card tilts slightly in 3D under the pointer.
6. **Product views:** real screenshots of the portal and the tablet in device frames (laptop and tablet), with depth
   and a parallax on scroll; captions per language.
7. **Figures:** measured numbers that count up when seen (300 tablets at a shift change, 72 h offline, 4.5 min to send
   400 entries on 3G), from the P2.12a results, never invented.
8. **For whom:** quarry owners, site managers, controllers, operators: what each one gains.
9. **Call to action:** a band with the two actions again.
10. **Footer:** product, company, legal (privacy, terms), contact, languages, © Karry.

Copy is written in French first, then English and Arabic by the owner or a translator; the agent drafts and marks
the drafts. No claim that the product does not do today.

## 3. Sign-in and sign-up

**Sign-in.** Each company has its own address (`<company>.karry.…`), and the sign-in page needs it (the company's logo
and colour, `redirect_uri`). **Se connecter** therefore opens a small dialog, "Your company's space", asking for the
company identifier (the slug, e.g. `carriere-demo-b`), remembers it on the device, and sends the person to
`https://<slug>.<domain>/`, which leads to the sign-in page. The identifier is checked against the anonymous branding
endpoint (`GET /api/v1/tenancy/public/{slug}`) to show the company's name before leaving; an unknown one says so,
without listing companies (rate-limited as today, residual R-07).

**Sign-up: owner decision needed.** Companies are created today by Karry operators (operator console, 30-day trial).
Options:

| Option | What happens | Work |
|---|---|---|
| A (recommended for the pilot) | **Créer un compte** opens a "Request a trial" form (company, country, contact, sites, machines); the request appears in the operator console; the operator creates the company and its first administrator gets the invitation e-mail | A public, rate-limited endpoint and a request list in the operator console (one slice) |
| B | Self-service: the form creates the company and its trial at once, the person sets their password and second factor | Abuse protection (e-mail check, captcha alternative, limits), billing rules; better after the pilot |
| C | **Créer un compte** is a "Contact us" link (e-mail) until there is a form | None |

## 4. Architecture

- **A new app `src/frontend/apps/site`** in the monorepo: Vite + React + TypeScript like the portal, sharing
  `@karry/ui` (tokens, buttons, focus styles) and `@karry/i18n` (a `site.*` namespace in the catalogs, checked by
  `check:catalogs`).
- **Prerendered:** one static HTML page per language (`/`, `/en/`, `/ar/`) built at build time; React hydrates the
  interactive parts. Served as static files (CDN or the same web server as the apps) with the shared security headers.
- **Motion:** CSS and SVG for most effects (scroll reveal with `IntersectionObserver`, tilt, count-up, the drifting
  arrows); a small motion helper, no heavy animation library.
- **3D:** the hero's K is a low-poly mesh built from the logo's geometry and drawn with **three.js**, loaded lazily after
  the first paint and only where WebGL is available, paused when off screen and with reduced motion. Version per the
  supply-chain rules (at least 7 days old, pinned, licence MIT). Fallback: the animated SVG logo.
- **Brand:** the logo files and the navy, blue and cyan of the sign-in page; the same fonts as the apps (Inter, Noto
  Sans Arabic).

## 5. Tests (every slice, in CI)

| Kind | What it proves |
|---|---|
| Unit (Vitest) | Language routing and switch, the company-space dialog (slug format, unknown company, remembered choice), count-up and reveal helpers, reduced-motion paths |
| Component and accessibility (Vitest + axe) | Every section in FR, EN and AR: no serious or critical axe violation, headings in order, links and buttons named, contrast |
| End to end (Playwright, local stack) | The page in each language; RTL mirrored in Arabic; **Se connecter** → company → the restyled sign-in page of that company → signed in to its portal; **Créer un compte** per the chosen option; keyboard-only path; no console error; the page policy blocks nothing |
| Visual regression | Screenshots of each section at 375, 768, 1366 and 1920 px in the three languages, compared on every PR (animations frozen) |
| Performance | Lighthouse CI with the budgets of §1 (fails the job when over) |
| 3D fallback | The hero without WebGL and with reduced motion shows the still logo; no error |
| Security | Headers checked like the apps' (`security-headers.spec.ts`); ZAP baseline nightly |
