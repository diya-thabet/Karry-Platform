# P2.10 notifications foundation: slice plan

> [Docs index](../../README.md) › [04-phases](../README.md) › [Phase 2 plan](plan.md) · [Sub-phases](sub-phases.md#p210--notifications-foundation) · [Tracker](tracker.md)

**In plain words:** people learn what concerns them. The operator when an entry is rejected, the manager when a
machine is unsafe or reviews are late: in the app, in a daily e-mail and, where the phone allows, a push message.
Design: [ADR-0039](../../adr/0039-notifications.md).

## 1. Scope

| Story | ACs in P2.10 |
|---|---|
| US-P2-NOT-01 Notification centre | AC1 (portal and field app, read/unread, deep links), AC2 (rules event → audience → channels, preferences per person), AC3 (e-mail for digests) |
| US-P2-OFF-04 Status pull | AC2 (Web Push tells the operator of a rejection, where supported) |
| US-P2-VER-06 SLA | AC2 (daily digest to the site manager: pending, overdue, corrected) |
| Producers | FO-02 AC3 (machine unsafe), FO-08 AC2 (fraud suspected), RULE-FO-08 (missing end) |

Tests: TC-P2-NOT-001 (a rejection reaches the operator's tablet after the next pull), TC-P2-NOT-002 (a manager of
site A gets nothing about site B; tenant isolation), TC-P2-NOT-003 (digest content and time zone), TC-P2-NOT-004 (no
masked field in a notice for a role that may not see it).
Done when: a rejection reaches the operator's tablet and a digest reaches Mailpit.

## 2. Slices

Each slice is one PR, tested, CI green, docs swept (ADR-0039 "as built", tracker, hand-off).

| # | Slice | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Notifications module: schema (RLS), default rules in code, outbox subscribers for the five events, audiences through Identity contracts, `GET /notifications` (ETag), mark read and read all (TC-P2-NOT-002): `INotices` in Notifications.Contracts, producers' subscribers in Verification, FieldOps and Integrity, Identity `INoticeAudiences`, `GET /notifications/mine`; the three producers' outboxes are now dispatched by the Workers | ✅ #248 |
| 2 | Portal notification centre: bell with unread count, list, deep links, polling with `If-None-Match`: bell in the header (a link, the count in its name), `/notifications`, every 30 s, links only to the portal's own pages | ✅ #249 |
| 3 | Field app: the unlocked operator's notifications through the pull (sync source, replace per person), shown on the home screen (TC-P2-NOT-001): `notices` sync source (the site's rejections, 14 days, replace), offline v8 (`notices`, `noticeReads` kept on the tablet), "For you" on the home screen | ✅ #250 |
| 4a | Preferences (channels per event, per person) and the company's rule switches; masking check on every notice's parameters (TC-P2-NOT-004): API, `rule_settings` (ledgered, step-up to switch off) and `preferences` (digest and push only; in-app always on), parameter allow-list per rule | ✅ #252 |
| 4b | Portal: the person's channel choices on the notifications page; the company's rule switches in Settings (administrator): "How you are told" under the list (digest and push per kind, in-app not offered), Settings › Notification rules (who hears, by which channels, switch with step-up to turn off, who changed it) | ✅ #253 |
| 5a | E-mail: `IEmailSender` (MailKit, Mailpit locally), delivery records with retries: the port in BuildingBlocks (`Messaging`), its SMTP adapter in Identity (which owns the sender settings), `email_deliveries` (person not address, backoff 1/5/15/60/240 min, given up after 6 or at once without an address), `EmailDeliveryJob` every minute | ✅ #254 |
| 5b | Daily digest at 07:00 site time to the site's managers (TC-P2-NOT-003): the digest-channel notices of the last day, and the review counts of US-P2-VER-06 AC2 (pending, overdue, corrected), bilingual, queued once per person, site and day: `DailyDigests` (07:00–12:00 local, NodaTime; yesterday 07:00 to today 07:00 local), Verification `IReviewCounts`, Identity `IAppAddresses`, Workers `DigestJob` every 15 minutes | 🟡 in review |
| 6 | Web Push behind `notifications.web-push`: VAPID keys, subscription per device and person, the service worker shows "something needs a look" | ⬜ |
| 7 | Close-out: E2E rejection → tablet and digest → Mailpit, 90-day retention job, security review (T-09, T-10, T-18), ADR-0039 accepted | ⬜ |
