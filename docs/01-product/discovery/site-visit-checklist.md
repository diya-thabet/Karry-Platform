# Site Visit Observation Checklist

> [Docs index](../../README.md) › [01-product](../README.md) › [discovery](README.md)

**Goal:** observe real work at ≥ 2 quarries, including one shift change. Produce **as-is process maps** and UX constraints for the field app. Take photos (with permission) of forms, meters, indicators, tanks, stores, network spots.

## Before the visit
- [ ] Permission for photos and short videos; safety induction and PPE booked
- [ ] Bring a test tablet with the clickable prototype, offline
- [ ] Phone with a signal-strength app to map 4G/Wi-Fi coverage

## Observe and record

| Area | Observe | Capture |
|---|---|---|
| **Shift change** | Who records start/end hours, where, how long it takes, who checks | Paper forms (photo), meter types and readability, lighting, gloves |
| **Machines** | Hour meter / odometer location and condition, broken meters | Photos of meters (reading difficulty), QR label placement options |
| **Fuel** | Tank/bowser, dispenser totalizer, dip stick, who fills which machine, records | Calibration table existence, receipt slips, reconciliation practice |
| **Weighbridge** | Truck flow, first/second weighing, ticket printing, cancellations | Indicator brand/model/port (RS-232/485/TCP), PC, printer, queue times per truck |
| **Plant** | Crushers, screens, conveyors, belt scales, stoppages and how they are logged | Flow sketch (for plant graph), nameplates (capacity), stockpiles |
| **Stores** | Central vs site store, issue process, transfer documents, counting | Item labels, bins, paperwork, who holds keys |
| **Maintenance** | Workshop, work orders, wear-part replacement records | Most-replaced parts, lead times, supplier locations |
| **Connectivity** | Signal at office, weighbridge, workshop, fuel point, plant, pit | Coverage map with dBm / "none" |
| **Devices** | Existing phones/tablets, charging points, dust/water exposure | Device models, Android versions |

## UX constraints to note
- Sunlight glare, dust, rain, gloves, noise, literacy, language(s) spoken, shared devices, time pressure at shift change.

## Output
1. As-is BPMN maps: shift start/end, fueling, weighing, store issue, transfer, maintenance stop.
2. Coverage map and device inventory.
3. Hardware list for the pilot (indicator model and port, printer, PC), which feeds ADR-0016.
4. List of "surprises" that change the backlog.
