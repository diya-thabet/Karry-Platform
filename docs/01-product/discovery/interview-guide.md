# Customer Discovery Interview Guide

> [Docs index](../../README.md) › [01-product](../README.md) › [discovery](README.md)

**Goal:** ≥ 8 interviews across segments S1–S4 ([business model](../business-model-and-pricing.md)). Learn the real pains and current workarounds, and how much a fix is worth. **Do not pitch** before the last 10 minutes.

**Interviewees:** owner/GM, site manager, controller/foreman, weighmaster, storekeeper, CFO/accountant. Record (with consent) or take verbatim notes.

## Interview flow (45 min)

| Minutes | Topic | Questions |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Context | Company, sites, machines (count/types), products, tonnes/month, crews and shifts, connectivity on site |
| 5–15 | Shift & fuel | "Walk me through yesterday's shift from start to end of paperwork." How are hours recorded? Who checks them? How is diesel dispensed and recorded? When did you last suspect hours or fuel were wrong? What did it cost? |
| 15–22 | Weighbridge & sales | How is a truck weighed and invoiced? Can tickets be edited or cancelled? Who can do it? Disputes with customers? m³ or tonnes? |
| 22–28 | Maintenance & stores | Last unplanned breakdown: what broke, how long was the stop, how long to get the part? How do parts move between central store and sites? When was the last stock count and what did it show? |
| 28–33 | Finance | Do you know the cost per hour and per tonne of each machine? Are machines financed by loans? How do you prepare for bank meetings? Effect of the rainy season? |
| 33–38 | Tools & budget | Current tools (paper, Excel, ERP, telematics)? What did you try that failed, and why? Who decides on software purchases? Budget per month for tools today? |
| 38–45 | Reaction | Show 3 prototype screens. "Which would you use tomorrow? What is missing? What would stop you?" Ask for referral and pilot interest. |
